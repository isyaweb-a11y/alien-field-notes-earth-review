## The entry

`main.py` writes one line to `/output/entry.txt` — an alien tourist's review of Earth:

> ★★★☆☆ Earth: gorgeous views, lovely locals, but the 'five-minute walk' took forty minutes and everything runs on caffeine and queues. Worth one visit. Bring snacks.

A real review: stars for the verdict, a complaint only a tourist would make, and the punch at the end.

python3 main.py
